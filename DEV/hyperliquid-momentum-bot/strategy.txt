Hyperliquid Momentum Bot Strategy
================================

Market:
- Hyperliquid perpetual futures
- Default symbol: BTC
- Default timeframe: 15m

Core structure:
- EMA 8  → short-term momentum
- EMA 21 → short/medium trend
- EMA 55 → medium trend
- EMA 89 → medium/long trend
- EMA 233 → major trend
- VWAP   → session value reference

Long entry conditions:
1. Price > VWAP
2. EMA 8 > EMA 21 > EMA 55 > EMA 89 > EMA 233
3. RSI(14) > 45 and rising vs previous candle
4. MACD histogram > 0 and rising vs previous candle
5. StochRSI K leaves oversold zone (< 0.20) and turns upward

Optional short entry conditions:
1. Price < VWAP
2. EMA 8 < EMA 21 < EMA 55 < EMA 89 < EMA 233
3. RSI(14) < 55 and falling
4. MACD histogram < 0 and falling
5. StochRSI K leaves overbought zone (> 0.80) and turns downward

Risk model:
- Position size based on account balance and configured risk percentage
- Stop-loss = ATR(14) × multiplier
- Take-profit = stop distance × reward/risk ratio

Monitoring:
- Terminal dashboard updates every 10 seconds
- Shows live price, VWAP, EMA cascade, RSI, MACD histogram, StochRSI and ATR
- Shows open position status and time remaining until next candle

Purpose:
- Capture pullbacks inside a confirmed trend
- Avoid counter-trend entries
- Improve trade quality using multi-layer confirmation
